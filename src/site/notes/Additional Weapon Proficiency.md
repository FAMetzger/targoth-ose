Characters improve their skill with individual weapons through regular and meaningful use.

Each weapon tracks its own training independently, and only the selected weapons may advance in rank.

Characters advance in rank as they accumulate practice and reach the appropriate character level, detailed on the table below according to the following principles:

##### Training Days: 
Each day in which the weapon is used effectively in combat or practiced for at least an hour counts as a meaningful use (note character cannot spend more than 2hrs/day in training). A character may record only one meaningful use per in-game day.

##### **Rank Advancement:** 
A character’s skill with a weapon increases through several ranks. Advancement requires meeting the listed number of training days for that rank (see chart below) and reaching a specific character level. 


Characters may train on one weapon per day

• Martial Classes: May advance in up to four additional weapons.
• Semi-Martial Classes: May advance in up to two additional weapons.
• Non-Martial Classes: May advance in one additional  weapon.
