**Frostwane** – deep winter; the cold begins to relent.

 **Thawreach** – rivers break their ice, and travel resumes.

**Bloomtide** – fields awaken and flowers return.

 **Highseed** – planting season and spring festivals.

**Suncrest** – early summer; the sun reigns long in the sky.

**Brightcall** – time of heat and celebration.

 **Emberfall** – late summer when fires burn low and crops ripen.

 **Harvestmere** – the season of gathering and plenty.

**Duskwane** – twilight comes sooner; leaves begin to fall.

**Graveveil** – misty, mournful days marking the year’s decline.

**Snowmourn** – first snows and long nights.

**Deeprest** – the dark of winter; hearthfires and quiet reflection.
