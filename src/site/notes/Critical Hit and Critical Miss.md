## **Critical Hit and Critical Miss**

Natural 20 Choice of
1. Do maximum damage. 
2. Trip (allows characters to move by freely, allows you to move freely, allows people to move over creature - movement treated as difficult terrain. You pick square creature falls into (Only 5ft adjacent and must be open). Prone creature can only attack with light weapons. 
3. Disarm (one held item). Retrieval of a dropped weapon is a full round action.

Natural 1- Roll1d10
Roll a 1- Opponent you are attacking gets counterstrike
Roll a 2-3- Trip, fall in randomly determined adjacent square 
Roll a 4-5- Drop Weapon Retrieval of a dropped weapon is a full round action.
Roll 6 and above- nothing
