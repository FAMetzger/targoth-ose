[[Magic/Magic\|Magic]]
Sleep 

Range: 120ft 

Area of Effect- Special 

Number of Creatures impacted: Special   

Need line of sight on area it is cast. It acts like a fire ball, so it will impact creatures you cant see (i.e., around the corner)   

Area of Effect/Targets: 

Can “call your shot” targeting a single creature up to HD4+ Saving throw at -2 vs spell negates (this is similar mechanic to Hold spell).
	
30ft burst- impacts 3d4+Intel MOD HD worth of creatures Saving throw vs spell negates (most 1-2HD creatures need a 16 to save vs. spell)                  

60ft burst- impacts 3d4+Intel MOD HD worth of creatures Saving throw +2 vs spell negates 

Creatures > 5HD                               
Do not take the -2 save penalty Save vs spell at +2 per lvl over 4+HD                               
Fail- slowed 1d4 rds (move half speed, take half # of attacks, always gets at least one)                


Sleeping creatures                               
    Can be killed with bladed weapon 1rd action                               
    Awoken by shaking, damage, or magical counteraction.                               
    Creatures awakening can                                              
		    Get up, move                                             
		    Pick up weapon, get up.
		    Get up and attack only if using a natural weapon (bite/claw/breath) and target is adjacent.
	Tactically                              
		Unintelligent monsters will not try to wake up sleeping Intelligent monsters--- it will depend on following                          
			Experience with magic                                              
			Situational awareness  
			                                            
		Example of a no attempt Group of 1HD common kobold with little/n0experience with magic                   
		
		Example of when they would Group of 1ND common kobold has Sergeant Dog Face (2HD kobold guard, fresh in from the goblin wars)  in the group and he isn’t impacted by spell. He will know the score and try to wake some his troops, if he has seen/heard spell cast.
