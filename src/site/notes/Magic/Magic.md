# **Start with**
One free cantrip 
	Sense Magic

Two free spells in spell book- 
	Detect Magic
	Read Magic


# **Spells per day**
According to progression chart but get bonus spells based on intel modifier
		Intel Bonus +1- an extra 1st level spell
		Intel Bonus +2- an extra 2nd level spell (when able to cast 2nd level spells)
		Intel Bonus +3 an extra 3rd level spell (when able to cast 3rd level spells)
		
You can NOT use bonus slots to double up on a single spell. You can use regular progression slots to double up

Example:
Alrec the Mage with an intel of 13 (+1 Intel) bonus
	At first level his spellbook has 
		3 Cantrips (Two plus his freebie)
		5 spells (2 freebies Detect Magic, Read Magic plus 3 from his intel modifier)
			He can cast 2 spells a day, but they cant be duplicates (you cant use bonus spell to double up)
	At second level
		He adds spells with a 70% success rate 
		He can now case 3 spells a day. If he chooses, he can use both non-bonus slots for same spell


# **Casting is a full round action**
 
 Must declare casting before you roll for initiative

**If hit while casting**- roll intelligence check +damage (1d20+Damage) 
		Pass- spell goes off
		Fail- lose spell
			Remember ability check is roll d20, lower passes, so addition to score is a penalty, subtraction is a benefit


# Identification of Magical Items

Potions can be tasted/smelt 

Sense Magic (Cantrip) will tell you if an item within 30 ft of you is magic (you need to be able to see item). It will not tell you what the item actually does


**Identification of Specific Enchantments**

Quick identification while in dungeon takes full turn, rolls intelligence check at a +4 point penalty. 
	
Identification without detect magic- Magic user needs to examine item for an hour, after that makes an intelligence check pass will identify item, fail will cause misidentification

Detect Magic will always tell what type of enchantment is on item  


# Scrolls 
Need to cast read magic to read scroll, once read can be used any time.

Using scrolls at a higher level at +1 penalty per level

Example: Alric, 1st level mage tries casting 3rd lvl Fireball
			He has 16 Intel, penalty is +2 (he needs a 14 or lower to successfully use scroll)
	
# Learning Spells 
Percent change of learning new spell (e.g., copying it in book) is based on intel modifier (pg 60 [[OSE Rules/OSE Advanced Characters\|OSE Advanced Characters]]  ). 

You get one chance per level to level spell.
