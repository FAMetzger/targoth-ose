#### Becoming Proficient: 
Characters become proficient in a field through meaningful involvement, either working on the field, learning with an expert or studying for at least 1 hour a day (can not spend more than 2 hrs a day training).

After accumulating a specific number of days, the character then becomes proficient.

#### Number of days required: 
At least 90 days of meaningful involvement are required to gain a specific proficiency.



#### Level Requirements: 
Additional to the days required, a character must be of 2nd level to acquire their first proficiency, additional proficiencies are gained in levels 4th, 6th and 8th, for a maximum of four proficiencies per character.

#### **General Proficiencies**
1 History Knowledge of major events, cultures, and timelines.
2 Geography Understanding of lands, borders, climates, and map interpretation.
3 Politics Knowledge of noble houses, symbols, and political lineages.
4 Philosophy Logic, ethics, metaphysics, and schools of thought.
5 Mathematics Arithmetic, geometry, and abstract reasoning.
6 Botanic Theoretical knowledge of flora, fauna, and ecosystems.
7 Anthropology Customs, traditions, and social structures of various peoples.
8 Bureaucracy Knowledge of administrative systems, laws and the internal logic of governments or institutions


#### **Cleric Proficiencies**
1 Theology Study of divine hierarchies, doctrines, and sacred teachings.
2 Mythology Knowledge of creation stories, heroic sagas, and divine narratives.
3 Iconography Understanding of sacred symbols, motifs, and religious imagery.
4 Ethics Study of moral systems, virtues, and ethical dilemmas. It helps a character analyze right and wrong through structured reasoning.
5 Cosmology Knowledge of spiritual realms, afterlives, and divine planes.
6 Demonology Study of fiendish hierarchies, contracts, and infernal lore.
7 Angelology Knowledge of celestial beings, their duties, and their manifestations.
8 Sacred Languages Study of ancient or divine tongues used in holy texts.
9 Relic Lore Knowledge of holy artifacts, their origins, and their significance.
10 Ritual Studies Understanding of the structure, symbolism, and purpose of sacred rites.
11 Eschatology Study of end‑times doctrines, apocalyptic visions, and final‑judgment beliefs.
12 Sacred Geometry Understanding of symbolic mathematical pattern

#### **Fighter Proficiencies**
1 Military Ranks Hierarchical structures within armies, including titles, duties, and chains of command.
2 Battle Tactics Battlefield maneuvers, formations, and engagement strategies.
3 Siege Tactics Offensive and defensive strategies used during sieges.
4 Armor Lore Knowledge of armor types, materials, and construction principles.
5 Weapons Lore Study of weapon designs, classifications, and the philosophies behind their use.
6 Logistics Understanding of supply chains, provisioning, and the movement of troops and resources.
7 Martial Arts Knowledge of combat traditions, stances, and theoretical principles.
8 Heraldry Coats of arms, banners, and symbolic markings used by noble and military houses.
9 Horsemanship Horse taming, raising, breeding and riding principles.
10 Fortifications Knowledge of defensive structures, their design logic, construction techniques and their strategic purpose.
11 Laws of War Study of codes, customs, and ethical frameworks governing conflict.
12 Beast Lore Knowledge of dangerous creatures, their instincts, ecology and their behavioral patterns.

#### **Magic-User Proficiencies**
1 Arcane Theory Study of the fundamental principles that govern magical energy, ley lines, and arcane interaction.
2 Alchemy Knowledge of symbolic transmutation, elemental correspondences, and the philosophical underpinnings of alchemical change.
3 Planar Cosmology Study of the structure, laws, and relationships between the various planes of existence.
4 Runes and Sigils Understanding of magical symbols, their encoded meanings, and the logic behind their construction.
5 Magical Constructs Knowledge of artificially created magical entities, bindings, and arcane frameworks.
6 Spellcraft Study of how spells are designed, layered, and balanced within the arcane system.
7 Astrology Knowledge of celestial bodies, constellations, and their influence on magical currents.
8 Enchantments Understanding of the theory behind imbuing objects or beings with magical properties.
9 Divination Methods used to perceive hidden truths, patterns, or future possibilities.
10 Curses and Hexes Knowledge of malefic magical effects, their symbolic structures, and the principles that bind them.
11 Minor Arcana Understanding of the inner workings of the mind and consciousness
12 Major Arcana Understanding of the physical and chemical rules and phenomena of the universe.

#### **Thief Proficiencies**
1 Forgery The methods, materials, and stylistic cues used in creating false documents.
2 Musical Instrument Knowledge of how to play one specific instrument.
3 Urban Lore Understanding of city layouts, social strata, and the hidden rhythms of urban life.
4 Criminality Illicit economies, gang structures, and the logic behind unlawful behavior.
5 Smuggling Covert transport routes, concealment strategies, and the economics of contraband and how to engage in it.
6 Poisons and Toxins Knowledge of harmful substances, their symptoms, how and where to obtain them.
7 Game Theory Study of probability, strategy, and decision‑making in competitive scenarios.
8 Con Artistry Confidence schemes, psychological manipulation, and the structure of classic swindles and scams.
9 Merchant Customs Understanding of trade etiquette, financial practices and commercial expectations across cultures.
10 Gem Appraising Knowledge of gemstone types, grading principles, and theoretical valuation.
11 Disposing Knowledge regarding how the networks, intermediaries, and methods used to make items or people “disappear”.
12 Lip Reading How to interpret speech by observing mouth movements and facial cues.
