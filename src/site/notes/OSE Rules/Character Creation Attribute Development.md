# Attribute Development

**Option 1**
·         Start by rolling down 4d6, pick top 3 die rolls, roll this x6
·         Add +2 to one stat of your choice (no scores above 18)
·         Assign rolls to Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma

**Or**

**Option 2**
·         Consider the following preset attribute scores which can be moved around as you wish.
		o   18, 16, 10, 8, 4, 3
		o   16, 14, 10, 9, 6, 5
		o   12, 12, 10, 10, 9, 9

**Or**

**Option 3**
For those who like a challenge- Rules as written. Roll 3d6, Assign rolls in order- Strength, Intelligence, Wisdom, Dexterity, Constitution, Charisma


# Hit Points

Level 1 max dice roll lvl 1, (i.e. 1d6=6) plus/minus con modifier

Subsequent Levels-> half max die roll +1 (i.e. 1d6=4) plus/minus CON modifier
	Can roll once to try to beat that (unless human then try x2)
