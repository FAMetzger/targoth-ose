Death at -HP greater than CON (e.g, if Con=12, death at -13)

The in between (0 to death)- unconscious

Every round under 0, roll save vs death.
	Pass – add CON Modifier until reach 0
	Fail--- take DAM1d6-CON modifier  

Can stabilize/stop process with field dressing (one turn to apply)


New Character
	Comes in at the average XP of the company
	 If greater than lvl 1 start with 210g
