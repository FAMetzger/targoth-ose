**Total Carry Capacity**

Average Strength (9-12)= 1500 coin weight 

STR Modifier for every +/-1, add/subtract 250 coin weight  

Example: 
	Jor has strength of 18 (+3)= Total carry capacity is 2250 coin = 1500+(3x250) 
	Alex has STR of 3 (-3) total carry capacity is 750 coin= 1500-(3X250) 

**Group based encumbrance (you can move as fast as your slowest member)** 

Add 
	Armor 
	Weapons 
	Gear, unless you are carrying an inordinate amount of gear, assume all mundane gear is 80 coin 
	Coin 
	Jewelry, gems, rings don’t count unless you are carrying a shit ton 

GM will track max carry capacity and total wight carried by group 

# **Movement** 
Carrying less than 25% of total capacity= 40ft per rd, 120ft per turn, 24miles/day 
Carrying between 26% and 50% total capacity= 30ft per rd, 90ft per turn, 18miles/day 
Carrying between 51% and 75% total capacity= 20ft per rd, 80ft per turn, 16 miles/day 
Carrying above 75% of total weight = 10ft per rd, 40ft per turn, 8 miles per day 

This assumes you are spreading treasure equally amongst part members. If you want to retain movement bonuses and not carry coin, you need to let DM know.
