Successful hit from undead. 
	Save vs death. 
		Fail take 1d6 CON damage 
		Reduced to 0 con – death and return as the type of creature that killed you unless efforts are made to prevent it.  
		
If you have not died after three hits from an intelligent undead creature that drains constitution, save vs spell. 
	Fail= become a thrall. 


Constitution Damage can only be restored in a place of Lawful worship with a remove curse spell (Cleric Lvl 6). Cost of oils, incense, religious bric-a-brac. 100gp/point restored.
