Monster and NPC morale will be checked, but only when it would reasonably make sense.
	You bust into a room, get surprise on a bunch of creatures, kill a few.
	The battle is clearly lost/going sideways

For Hirelings
	Dropping of the guy who pays his bills is an immediate morale check
	For noncombatant NPC- taking damaged/being attacked is automatic check.   

Creatures that are broken will 
	Drop what they carry, 
	Stop defending themselves (e.g., they wont attack, you can move through them/by them with no issue)
	Flee area. 
		If there is no chance of escape, they will resume attack, using what weapons are at hand
		
Party gets XP for creatures that run from battle and live.
