# **Nets/Bolas**

If creature fails save, entangled for the rest of the round and until next round during initiative order (when they get another save with +2 modifier, additional +2 modifier each successive round)

**Ally can cut the effected person free, but**
1.      Takes full action (i.e., cant cut friend lose and then attack)
2.      Unintelliegent creatures wont try to free allies.
3.      Half damage to the entangled creature.
4.      Creature can cut themselves free
		If they have a natural weapon that would make sense to do this (claws, but not necessarily bite, unless mouth is in weird spot)


**How many can you carry?**
	Nets- 1
	Bolas -3


**When do you lose them or when do they get destroyed?**

Lose only when you throw, miss, and line of attack sends bolas off a cliff, into the water etc.

Creatures cutting there way free does not destroy the bola (easily repaired)     



# **Whip**

If creature fails save, entangled for the rest of the round and until next round during initiative order (when they get another save with +2 modifier, additional +2 modifier each successive round)

**Ally can cut the effected person free, but**
1.      Takes full action (i.e., cant cut friend lose and then attack)
2.      Unintelliegent creatures wont try to free allies.
3.      Half damage to the entangled creature or they can attack whip (need to hit your                               unarmored AC with bladed weapon)
4.      Creature can cut themselves free

If they have a natural weapon that would make sense to do this (claws, but not necessarily bite, unless mouth is in weird spot)

**When do you lose the whip/when do they get destroyed?**

Lose only when  it is taken from you and you can’t get back.

Creatures cutting their way free does not destroy the whip, but need  an hour to repair


# **Non standard sized opponents (non-humanoid, large, strong, etc.)**

**Call Shot**, “I am going for legs or trying to pick off a weapon, entangle an arm.” Attack is made at -2

Or 

**Let Fly** a50/50 as to whether it impacts movement or attack.

Applying to various creatures will be case by case.

Example 
	You might not be able to completely stop the giant spider, but can slow. 
	You aren’t going to be able to stop a hill giant's attack, but can interfere with it.


# **Held- Grabbed**
Most creatures will do auto damage on the round after they grab and hold you (crush, strangulation, bite). 

At the start of the creatures turn held-grabbed character gets a saving throw vs paralysis. 
	Succeed = break free, no auto damage, but creature still gets full attack
	Fail = take auto damage

 At start of held=grabbed creatures turn can try to save vs paralysis (this is full round action) or attack with a light weapon (dagger)
