Pick a deity (see [[Religion/Pantheon\|Pantheon]] or [[Religion/Additional Religions\|Additional Religions]])

##### **Translate tenants of faith and answer following questions**

Are you mandated to kill a creature that challenges you?

Are you mandated to kill or spare an opponent that had surrendered after coming to blows with you or your party?

Are you mandated to kill or spare an opponent that had surrendered after injuring you or your party?

Are you mandated to kill or spare an opponent that had surrendered before coming to blows with you or your party?

Are you mandated to intervene if a less powerful creature/person is in need?



#### **Conduct**
You are responsible for party members violating a tenant of your faith. Violation done with your knowledge/implicit condonement, i.e., “ I am going for a walk,” is a violation of faith

Minor violations incur minor disfavor from the God, i.e., One of your party members kills a helpless creature and you do nothing. Take a -1 to healing power until you atone (church donation/time spent in prayer.)

Multiple minor violations can turn into major ones. Major violations of your tenant (you kill a group of innocent kobolds) will result in loss of paladin powers. May be restored (for considerably more gold and time than the minor ones)



**NOTE-** It is impossible for two paladins of diametrically opposed tenants to adventure together without one incurring the wrath of his/her god.

##### **Warhorse** 

<U>Not given at 4th lvl.</U>

At 4th lvl and beyond, Paladin may declare an animal his war horse. An intelligent creature needs to agree. Animal must be able to bear weight of paladin.    

Paladins cannot exchange mounts- What you pick is yours till it or you dies. If mount dies, you can pick an entirely different creature. If you intentionally kill you mount (DM discretion determines this), you are fallen until you properly atone
