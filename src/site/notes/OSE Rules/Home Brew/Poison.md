# **Poison From Animals** 

Poison damage is always = to bite damage. 

Save vs poison- if fail 
	Onset is in 1d4 turns. 
	Duration is for 1d4 turns. 

No additional saving throws unless there is intervention aka Barbarian’s Cure Poison ability. 

Additional bites and failed saving throws reduce onset and increase duration by 1. 
	**Example** 
		Thrag gets bit by Crab Spider for 1d8 bite damage and fails his save. 
		Rolls 1d4 =2 
			Onset is a 2 (starts 2 turn after combat)
			Duration is a 2 turns of 1d8 poison.
		Sadly Thrag has 6HP after the bite. Do you watch him die or pour healing into him in hopes you can stay ahead of the poison?

There are magical poisons and special extracts. Onset and damage from these vary.
