# **Poison
 

**Damage**
	Immediately take Poison Damage - CON MOD

**Duration**
	1d4 to 1d20 rds of poison damage (depending on strength)
			1HD 1d4
			2HD 1d6
			3HD 1d8
			4HD 1d10
			5HD 1d12
			6HD 1d20
			7HD 1d20+ 1 per HD above 6

No additional saving throws unless there is intervention aka Barbarian’s Cure Poison ability.
