Will be used, but only in situations where the other NPC/Monster has no reason to have an opinion about you. The initial reaction will then be modified based on your actions

Example-
	
Loren breaks into the Orc Chieftain’s bedroom, after killing some of his guards. Loren’s dazzling smile does nothing to stay the hand of the Orc, there is going to be a fight.

Zeb, the local jeweler, doesn’t know you from Adam. You roll reaction to determine how he feels about you (i.e., what mark up he is going to charge.) Changes to that rate are then negotiation and/or things you have done to build your reputation.
