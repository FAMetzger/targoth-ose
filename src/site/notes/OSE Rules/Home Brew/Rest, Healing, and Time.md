# **Rest**
Rest includes player, spell prep, item examination, etc.

In dungeon- Rest 10min for every 4hrs. (1 turn every 24)
	Rationale--- There is a lot of standing around in dungeon time,  your thief is checking an area for traps, you are standing guard while someone loots the room, etc.  


# **Healing**
Resting heals 1d3+CON modifier, provided following
-          You have a bedroll
-          You are protected from elements.


# **Time**
Turn = 10min

Round= 6 seconds

Travel overland calculated at hrs/days scale           

Dungeon Crawling – calculated at turn/round scale

Turns/Days will be tracked for purposes of torch/food consumption

Typical day is 12hrs activity/12 rest
