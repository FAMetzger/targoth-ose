1 turn of searching =10min 

Character can search all adjacent squares net to him/her
	Search Area includes 
		Trap Searching
		Secrete Door Searching 
		Loot Searching

Company can designate a specific person to search for traps
	Trap Searcher needs to specify what they are searching 

Company can designate a specific person to search for secrete doors. 
	Light search - must designate area
	Heavy search - can roll for whole room 
		 

# **Searching levels** 

**Light**- Check pockets, packs, drawers, open chests, under items- 1 Turn 

**Moderate**- Move furniture, sort through loose piles of items, behind paintings, etc.- 2 Turns 

**Heavy**- Tear place apart--- Check seams of clothing, find anything hidden in a mundane fashion, find things in weird places (prison pouch, disguised items, etc.)-3 turns Turns burn torches, count towards random monster checks. Heavy searching increases noise level, attracting/alerting creatures
