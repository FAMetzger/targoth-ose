In order to level up
- Need the appropriate XP
- 150gp X (Target Level-1)
	- Example 1st level figher moving to 2nd= 150gp = 150gp x 1 (LVL2-1) 
	- Example 3rd lvl fighter moving to 4th= 450gp= 150gp x3 (LVL4-1)
- Access to a higher level trainer (trainer needs to be at least the level you are trying to obtain)
- One week down time, no other activities
