Bleeding Sands of Makspear is an inhospitable, trackless expanse where compasses spin uselessly, footprints vanish within minutes, and dunes shift with unnatural purpose. Wandering here without a guide is a slow death.

The desert is composed of unnaturally red sand—fine as powdered glass and the color of fresh blood. When the wind rises, it drives the scarlet grains into spiraling storms that look like the sky itself is hemorrhaging. Travelers swear the sand sometimes darkens in the moonlight, as though freshened by unseen violence.

No one agrees on the desert’s origin. Some claim it is the site of a battle between gods. Others think the red hue is the leeching of something buried deep beneath.  
  

At the southern most border, where desert meets the roaring sea, lies the only true settlement: Saltwarden’s Warrens.
