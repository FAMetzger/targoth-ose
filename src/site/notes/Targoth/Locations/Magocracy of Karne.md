###### **Name:** 
Magocracy of Karne

###### **Capital:** 
Weavespire

###### **Description:** 
Magocracy of Karne needs no border strongholds to guard its lands, relying on the mystic powers of its leaders and impenetrable jungle the comprise all its land.

At its heart—both geographically and politically—lies Weavespire, a sprawling city built atop the ruins of an elder civilization whose secrets still hum beneath the cobblestones. Here rules the Council of Five, mages of formidable talent and even more formidable ambition.

Though they claim to guide the kingdom together, each believes themselves destined for supremacy and the realm is beset by schemes layered upon schemes, assassinations, spies, and shifting alliances.

Beyond the city’s safety, the jungle rules. Rumors abound of sunken ziggurats and places of power long forgotten and swallowed by the jungle, but guarded by colossal stone guardians. More disturbing tales of human sacrifice and dark rituals in the Council of Five secret strongholds.

Caravans travel only with armed escorts and rarely stray from established routes. The common people live as the expendable, knowing their fates ride on the whim of the mages, but also knowing that power and wealth beyond imagination await those who earn their favor.

###### **Ruling Structure:**
Council of Five
