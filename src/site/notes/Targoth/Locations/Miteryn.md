**Name:**  Miteryn


**Capital:** Shiverstow 


**Description:** The Kingdom stretches along a broad, temperate coastline where deep harbors meet fertile river plains. Sea trade and agriculture form twin pillars of the realm’s wealth, binding coast, river, and hinterland into a single economic web. Though the land is divided into semi-independent duchies—each dominated by a different race and culture—they are held together by oath, coin, and the will of the king.

The realm’s unity is pragmatic rather than ideological: no duchy thrives alone.


**Ruling Structure:** Monarchy King Rimzes Turvolun, a human of formidable reputation, rules from the capital port-city of **Shiverstow**. He is neither beloved nor hated, but feared for his decisiveness and respected for his ability to keep the duchies from open war. His power rests on three things:

- Control of the royal navy
    
- Authority over inter-duchy trade law and tariffs
    
- The ancient **Covenant of Grain and Salt**, which binds food supply and sea access under the crown
- 
The king allows broad local autonomy, so long as taxes flow, ships sail, and banners answer the call in times of war.


The realm borders the Wildlands and the people of Miteryn have  complicated relationship with races and creatures of the wildlands. Shifting alliances abound, as the practical among those who live on the border, realize that cooperation is the key to survival.
