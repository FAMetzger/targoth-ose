###### **Name:** 
Norrhollow

###### **Capital:** 
None

###### **Description:** 
Far beyond the settled kingdoms lies the Norrhollow,  the “Lid of Targoth,”, a brutal northern wasteland where winter never ends and the wind howls like a grieving spirit. Snow dunes swallow entire valleys, glaciers grind slowly across the land like living mountains, and daylight comes only in pale, reluctant hours.

Dozens of clans roam the tundra, each defined by its own rites, scars, and blood-oaths. Their warriors wrap themselves in winter wolf pelts, carve stories into bone totems, and speak in thunderous dialects shaped by the storms they were born into. Feuds as old as memory spark battles across the ice fields, and victories are celebrated with roaring fires that burn against the darkness.

These clans fight constantly—for territory, for honor, for survival, and for the favor of the ancient powers they revere. But for all their savagery, they are bound by a shared respect for two sacred places where no blood may be spilled.



**Mountain Frost Father**
To the east rises Mountain Frost Father, a colossal spire of ice-veined stone whose peak is forever lost in swirling blizzards. The clans believe the Frost Father is an ancient titan—slain or slumbering beneath the mountain—whose breath still births the cold winds.

Each Fall, clans ascend as far as the storms allow to offer to vie for leadership of all the tribes or, when that fails, formally declare hostilities with one another.



**Ice Mother**
Across the frozen sea lies the Ice Mother, a sweeping field of glacial shelves that rise into arches and caverns like a frozen cathedral. The clans claim she is the first spirit to shape the world from snow and shadow, and that every warrior’s soul returns to her at death to await rebirth.

At the spring thaw—what little thaw the Expanse knows—the clans send women folk to the Ice Mother in hopes that a birth on the mountain portends greatness for the clan.



**Seasonal Unity – The Ice Moot**  
When the Shattered Ice Sea freezes thick enough to bear armies, the tribes enact the ancient Ice Moot. Messengers are sent across the tundra carrying frost-marked staves, calling all tribes to temporary unity.  

- Feuds are suspended under pain of death
    
- A Winter-King is chosen, not by bloodline but by acclaim and feats of strength
    
- Raiding routes across the frozen sea are planned
    
- Spoils and captives are pre-divided to prevent bloodshed during the campaign
    

This unity lasts only as long as the ice holds. When spring thaws the sea, the Winter King’s authority ends—often violently.
