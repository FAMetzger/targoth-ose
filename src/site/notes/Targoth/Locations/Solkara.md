###### **Name:** 
**Solkara**

###### **Capital:** 
Arcfeld

###### **Description:** 
Once the radiant heart of the world, Solkara was a continent where spires of marble and crystal touched the clouds, streets glimmered with ever-burning lamps, and scholars walked side by side with archmages and priests. Its cities were so advanced that people told legends of sky-bridges that arced between mountains, and colossal libraries whose shelves rearranged themselves to suit the reader. Solkaran influence shaped commerce, diplomacy, and magic itself.

Then came the Paroxysm. The land itself crumbled away, seas rushed inward and what had been a vast continent became a broken land mass with jagged peninsulas and high plateaus clinging above the waves.

Now Solkara is a scared wasteland, its once-splendid horizon warped into ragged silhouettes. Ruined capitals lie half-buried in drifting ash-sand, and strange storms crackle with remnant magic. Towering monoliths—once engines of civilization—now stand as silent, humming obelisks, leaking unpredictable energies that twist the land around them.

And yet… people come.

Treasure hunters, scholars, zealots, mercenaries, and fools are drawn to Solkara in droves, dreaming of uncovering the unimaginable artifacts rumored to survive beneath the ruins: devices that could lift armies, rewrite fate, or awaken powers better left alone.

###### **Ruling Structure:**
Despite devastation, one line endured: House Valcaryn, remnants of the old imperial bloodline. Once rulers of a glittering empire, they now preside from Arcfeld, a battered citadel built atop the only known stable ground.
