A massive amalgamation of islands with a myriad of different cultures, religions. Largely seen as lawless and unexplored, culture and fantastical creatures.


The three most notable (well known islands)

**Aelstrom’s Gate** – permanent hurricane and a known ship graveyard.

**Korran’s Hold** – a volcanic fortress city controlling most lucrative sea trade routes.

**The Serpent’s Spine** – a long island ridge believed to be the body of a slain sea god and home to one of the largest dens of iniquity in Targoth.
